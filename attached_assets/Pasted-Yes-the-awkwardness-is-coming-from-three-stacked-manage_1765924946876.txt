Yes — the awkwardness is coming from three stacked manager inputs fighting horizontal space. The fix is to compress them into a tight, single-row “Managers on Duty” strip with short labels and equal widths.

Below is a drop-in layout change that keeps everything concise and visually balanced.

✅ New DMLE Manager Layout (clean + compact)
Design goals

One Managers on Duty row

Short labels: AM / PM / Kit

Equal-width inputs

No vertical stacking

Tight spacing, log-header feel

Horizontal alignment = faster scanning

Input-group labels save space (no extra text rows)
